Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database)

This application uses Google Firebase, a development platform provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We use the following Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication for user registration, login, and anonymous guest login. When you register, your email address, your password (stored by Google in encrypted form), and your display name are processed by Firebase Authentication.
  • Firebase Realtime Database for storing application data (user profiles, contacts, and tasks). The database is located in the Google Cloud region europe-west1 (Belgium, EU).

The Firebase JavaScript libraries required for this are loaded from Google servers (www.gstatic.com) when you access any page of this application. In the process, your IP address is transmitted to Google, as this is technically necessary to deliver the files to your browser. The legal bases are Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR (provision of the application functions you request) and Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR (secure and efficient provision of the website).

Google acts as our processor within the meaning of Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of Google's data processing terms. Although the database is located in the EU, individual services (such as Firebase Authentication and the delivery of the JavaScript libraries) may involve the transfer of data to Google LLC in the USA. This transfer is safeguarded by Google's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and by EU standard contractual clauses. Further information can be found in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and in Google's information on privacy and security in Firebase at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Registration and Guest Login

When you register an account, we process your name, your email address, and your password. The password is managed exclusively by Firebase Authentication; we never store it in the database. In addition, your name, email address, an automatically assigned avatar color, and the time of registration are stored in your user profile in the Realtime Database. The legal basis is Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR.

Alternatively, you can use the application via guest login. In this case, no name, email address, or password is collected; Firebase Authentication merely creates an anonymous user ID for the duration of your session.

Shared Demo Database — Important Notice

Join is a demonstration project with a shared database: all contacts and tasks are stored in a common data pool that is visible to and editable by all logged-in users, including guest users. Any data you enter (e.g., contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, task descriptions) can therefore be viewed, modified, or deleted by other users of this demo application.

For this reason, please do not enter any real personal data — neither your own beyond what is required for registration, nor data of third parties. Use fictitious data for testing purposes. If you enter personal data of third parties despite this notice, you are responsible for ensuring that you are permitted to do so.

Local Storage in Your Browser

This application stores technically necessary information in your browser's local storage and session storage (no cookies are set by us). This includes your login session (managed by the Firebase SDK), session markers for guest login and the current user, flags controlling the intro animation, and local caches of contact and task lists to speed up loading. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you request and is therefore permitted under Section 25 Para. 2 TDDDG; no consent is required. The entries are removed or become invalid when you log out or clear your browser data. No tracking or analytics tools are used on this website.

You can delete contacts and tasks yourself at any time within the application.
